READER'S NOTE

How the record works.

The terminal is a work of interactive fiction about manufactured history.

The creation of a person

In Part One, Chapter IV of Nineteen Eighty-Four, Winston is assigned to alter records after Comrade Withers has been erased from the Party's history. Orwell does not give him a standard character-creation form. Winston invents Comrade Ogilvy himself to fill the gap, giving him an exemplary life story that supports the Party's account.

This game turns that scene into a fictional profile workflow. Name, full birth date, description, occupation, origin, and biography are form fields created for this game; they are not a Ministry checklist quoted from the novel.

Full name: Mara Venn
Date of birth: August 19, 1954
Occupation: Records clerk
Place of origin: Airstrip One

Official description: A precise clerk praised for keeping public records orderly and legible.

Biography: Mara Venn served in the Records Division, where she organized public notices and corrected outdated entries. She trained new clerks to compare each document with its authorized copy. Colleagues remember her careful handwriting, steady judgment, and punctual reports. Her methods helped the central registry keep its files consistent.
